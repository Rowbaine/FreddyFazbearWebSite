Common Fixes Reference

Symptom Likely Cause First Step
Animatronic unresponsive Low battery or tripped safety lockout Check battery %, then attempt remote restart
Camera feed frozen or dark Loose cable or camera reboot needed Restart the feed from this panel before dispatching a tech
Servo lag or grinding noise Needs lubrication or minor calibration Run self-test, schedule inspection if it recurs
Room temperature drifting Vent blockage or HVAC cycling issue Check HVAC panel, inspect vents nearest the alert
Door sensor false alarm Sensor misalignment Re-sync sensor from the power & doors panel
